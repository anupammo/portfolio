**Result:** ~0-5 leads/month from potential 50-100/month

## CTA STRATEGY TRANSFORMATION

### From Generic To Strategic

| Generic | Strategic | Specific |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| "Contact Us" | "Get Free Consultation" | "Schedule 15-Min Strategy Call (Free)" |
| "Learn More" | "View Case Study" | "See How We Increased Revenue 240%" |
| "Get In Touch" | "Send Message" | "Chat With Our Team (Avg response: 2 min)" |
| "Get Started" | "Book Now" | "Reserve Your Q2 Project Slot" |

### CTA Placement (Per Page Type)
```
Homepage: 6-8 CTAs (primary + secondary)
Service pages: 5-6 CTAs each
Blog: 3-4 CTAs per post
Portfolio: 2-3 CTAs per case study
Contact: 2 CTAs (form + chat)
```

### CTA Testing (Monthly)
- Test button colors: Current purple vs. variations
- Test copy: 5 variations of primary CTA
- Test placement: Above/below fold, sidebar
- Test form fields: 3-field vs. 6-field version
- **Target:** 10-15% improvement per test iteration
