Core Features

Everything a Resort Front Desk Needs

Built with a working village resort in West Bengal, around how Indian resorts actually run: day outings and night stays, group bookings, veg and non-veg meals, UPI and GST.

Online Booking & Instant Quotes

Guests pick a day outing or night stay, the party, rooms and extras, and see the GST-inclusive total, advance and balance before booking.

UPI, Cash & Bank Payments

A UPI QR with the amount filled in on every voucher. Cash, UPI and bank transfers are recorded with a receipt voucher; the same UTR can't be entered twice.

GST Vouchers & Tax Invoices

Booking vouchers with your logo and policies, and numbered GST tax invoices with CGST and SGST by rate. Download as PDF, print or share on WhatsApp.

Multi-Night Stays

Check-in and check-out dates, 1 to 14 nights. Extend or shorten a stay later; rooms are checked and held for every new night, so nothing is double-booked.

Veg or Non-Veg by Night

Each night can be veg or non-veg. Change one night or all of them; the price difference is shown before saving.

Extras by Day

Add cold drinks, seasonal dishes or a music system for a particular day, at the rate-card price with GST. A wrong item comes off with one tap.

Today Screen & Kitchen Sheet

Who arrives, who's in house, each guest's meal tonight and money to collect. A printable kitchen sheet with veg and non-veg counts and items to prepare.

Owner Dashboard

Booking value, guests expected, payments to verify and the balance to collect, with 7, 30 and 90-day views.

Staff Roles & Activity Log

Reception, Manager, Owner and Super Admin each see only what they should. Every change is logged with who made it and when.

See It in Action

Real Screens, Built for Phones First

Screenshots from ResortDesk with sample bookings. Staff use it on a phone at the desk or a computer in the office; guests book from any phone.

GST booking voucher with dates on every line

Meals show the nights they're for ("2 adults × 1 night: 18 Nov"), extras show their day, and GST is split into CGST and SGST. Guests pay exactly what they were quoted.

  • Invoice and receipt numbers in the right financial-year series
  • Your logo, policies and UPI QR on every voucher
  • PDF, print or WhatsApp in one tap

Guests change their minds. So can the booking.

Switch a night to non-veg, extend the stay, or add drinks for tomorrow. ResortDesk shows the difference and the new total before anything is saved, and the voucher and kitchen sheet update straight away.

  • Booked rates are kept; a new meal uses today's rate card
  • Rooms are checked for each added night
  • Nothing changes after the tax invoice is issued

The kitchen always knows what to cook

The Today screen and the printable kitchen day sheet count veg and non-veg by each guest's meal for that date, and add up every item ordered for the day.

  • "Night 2 of 3" so long stays are never missed
  • Check in, verify ID and collect the balance at the desk
  • Calendar to close dates and set capacity

Guests book from their phone in minutes

A clean quote and booking page on your own address, with live availability, the advance to pay and your cancellation policy. Your website can show the same live prices and send enquiries straight to the staff screen.

  • Adult, child and free under-6 rates
  • Rooms per night, extras once
  • Guests get their own booking page with the voucher
