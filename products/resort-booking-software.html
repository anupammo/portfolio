Everything a resort front desk needs

Built with a working village resort in West Bengal, around how Indian resorts actually run: day outings and night stays, group bookings, veg and non-veg, UPI and GST.

Online booking and instant quotes

Guests choose a day outing or night stay, the party size, rooms and extras, and see the full GST-inclusive price, the advance and the balance before they book. Availability is checked for every night, so a full date or room can never be sold twice.

  • Day outings and night stays of 1 to 14 nights
  • Adult, child and free under-6 rates
  • Your own website shows the same live prices

GST booking vouchers, tax invoices and UPI payments

Every booking gets a professional voucher with your logo, policies and a UPI QR code that already has the amount in it. Managers issue numbered GST tax invoices with CGST and SGST by rate. Cash, UPI and bank transfers are recorded with a receipt voucher, and the same transaction reference can't be entered twice.

  • Download as PDF, print, or share on WhatsApp
  • Dates shown on every meal and item line
  • Invoice and receipt numbers in the right financial-year series

Meals by night, longer stays and extras by day

Guests change their minds. ResortDesk lets staff switch any night between veg and non-veg, extend or shorten a stay, and add cold drinks, seasonal dishes or a music system for a particular day. The price difference is shown before anything is saved.

  • Extend a stay: rooms are checked and held for each new night
  • Booked rates are kept; new meals use today's rate card
  • A wrong item comes off with one tap

Today screen and kitchen day sheet

Reception opens Today and sees who arrives, who is in house, each guest's meal for tonight and the money still to collect. The kitchen gets a one-page sheet with veg and non-veg counts for that date and an "items to prepare today" list.

  • Check in, mark ID verified and record cash at the desk
  • "Night 2 of 3" so nobody forgets a long stay
  • Calendar to close dates and set capacity

Owner dashboard, staff roles and a full activity log

Owners see booking value, guests expected and the balance to collect at a glance. Reception, Manager, Owner and Super Admin each see only what they should: only managers confirm bank payments, and only owners change bank details or prices. Every change is logged with who made it and when.

  • Bookings list in cards or table, with rooms, nights and status
  • Rate card, rooms, policies, logo and colours you control
  • Secure: HTTPS everywhere, signed sign-ins, role checks on every action
