How Much Does a Website Cost in India in 2026?

By Anupam Mondal · Updated July 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer: In 2026, a professional website in India typically costs ₹8,000–₹30,000 for a small-business site, ₹30,000–₹1,00,000 for a custom or CMS-based site, and ₹75,000+ for eCommerce or web applications. The final price depends on pages, design, features, and whether SEO and lead-capture are built in.
Key takeaways
  • Price is driven by scope, not by how many pages you can cram in.
  • Ultra-cheap sites (₹2,000–₹5,000) usually cost more later — no SEO, no support.
  • Budget for ongoing costs: domain, hosting, and optional maintenance.
  • Ask "will it bring customers?" — ROI matters more than the sticker price.

If you've asked three different developers what a website costs, you've probably got three wildly different numbers — from ₹3,000 to ₹3,00,000. Both can be "correct," because they're quoting completely different things. After 18+ years building websites for clients in India and abroad, here's an honest, no-nonsense breakdown so you can budget with confidence.

What actually drives the price?

  1. Design — a custom, on-brand design costs more than a ready template, but builds far more trust.
  2. Number & complexity of pages — a booking flow or portal costs more than a static page.
  3. Functionality — payments, logins, multi-language, integrations all add work.
  4. SEO & performance — a site engineered to rank and load fast is worth more than one that just exists.
  5. Content — copywriting, photos, and product data take time (yours or the developer's).
  6. Support & ownership — do you own the code and get help after launch, or are you locked in?

Why are some quotes so cheap (and why it matters)

A ₹3,000 website is usually a generic template with no SEO, no speed optimisation, no analytics, and no support — and sometimes you don't even own it. It looks fine on day one and then quietly fails to bring a single enquiry. I've rebuilt many of these, and the "cheap" route almost always ends up costing more. The goal isn't the lowest price; it's the best return on what you spend.

Don't forget ongoing costs

  • Domain: ₹800 – ₹1,500 / year
  • Hosting: ₹0 on static hosting (like GitHub Pages) up to ₹3,000 – ₹8,000 / year for managed hosting
  • Maintenance / updates: optional, ₹1,000 – ₹5,000 / month if you want it handled
  • SEO / marketing: optional but often the best ROI once the site is live

So, what should you actually budget?

For most small businesses in Kolkata and across India, a ₹20,000–₹40,000 budget gets a fast, professional, SEO-ready website that can genuinely bring in customers. Spend less only if you truly need something minimal; spend more when you're selling online or need custom features. Frequently asked questions

How much does a basic website cost in India in 2026?

Between ₹8,000 and ₹30,000 for a professional small-business site, depending on pages, design, and whether SEO and lead capture are included.

Why are some website quotes so cheap?

Very cheap quotes usually mean a template with no SEO, no optimisation, and no support — and often no ownership of the code. They rarely generate leads.

Does a website have ongoing costs?

Yes — plan for domain and hosting, plus optional maintenance or SEO.

Is a cheaper website worth it?
