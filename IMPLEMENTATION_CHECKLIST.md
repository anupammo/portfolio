## SUCCESS METRICS & KPIs

### Conversion Metrics (Track Weekly)
```
Lead Capture Rate = Leads ÷ Visitors
  Current: <1%
  Target Month 1: 1-2%
  Target Month 3: 2-3%

CTA Engagement Rate = CTA Clicks ÷ Visitors
  Target: 3-5%

Email Opt-In Rate = Subscribers ÷ Visitors
  Target: 1-2%

Form Completion Rate = Completed Forms ÷ Started Forms
  Target: 60-70%
```

### Email Metrics (Track Weekly)
```
Open Rate = Opened ÷ Delivered
  Industry benchmark: 20-25%
  Target: 25-30%

Click Rate = Clicked ÷ Opened
  Industry benchmark: 2-3%
  Target: 3-5%

Unsubscribe Rate = Unsubscribes ÷ Delivered
  Target: <0.5%

Conversion Rate = Actions ÷ Delivered
  Target: 5-10% (lead to consultation)
```

### Lead Quality Metrics (Track Weekly)
```
Lead Score = Qualification assessment (1-10)
  Target average: 6+/10

Sales Qualified Leads = Leads meeting criteria
  Target: 70%+ of leads

Time to Close = Days from lead to customer
  Target: 7-14 days

Customer Lifetime Value = Revenue ÷ Customers
  Target: $5,000-15,000
```

### Business Metrics (Track Monthly)
```
Cost Per Lead (CPL) = Marketing Spend ÷ Leads
  Target: <$50

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) = Total Spend ÷ New Customers
  Target: <$500

Return on Investment (ROI) = Revenue ÷ Spend
  Target: 300%+ by Month 4

Monthly Recurring Revenue = Repeat service revenue
  Target: $5,000-10,000
```
