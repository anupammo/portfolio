# 03 · SEO, Keyword & Content Strategy (2026)

Goal: **maximum impressions + organic buyers for minimum effort** by targeting terms with
*intent + local advantage + low competition*, and by writing content that both ranks in Google
and gets **cited by AI answer engines** (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot).

---

## 1. The 2026 organic playbook (what actually moves rankings now)

1. **E-E-A-T with first-hand Experience** — Google's helpful-content system rewards demonstrable
   experience. Your 18 years + shipped products + named clients are gold; put author bios,
   credentials, and real screenshots/results on everything.
2. **Topical authority via clusters** — one "pillar" page per pillar, 5–10 supporting posts, all
   interlinked. Depth on a narrow topic beats scattered one-offs.
3. **GEO / Answer Engine Optimization** — structure content so a machine can lift a clean answer:
   a 40–60 word direct answer near the top, definitions, bullet takeaways, tables, FAQ schema.
4. **Entity SEO** — consistent identity + `sameAs` + `LocalBusiness`/`Person` schema so search
   engines *know who you are* as an entity.
5. **Local intent** — location + service pages + Google Business Profile; the local pack is the
   fastest route to buyers for a Kolkata-based consultant.
6. **Core Web Vitals & UX signals** — fast LCP, low CLS, good INP; you're static so this is easy to win.
7. **Genuine backlinks/mentions** — from your own tool listings (WordPress.org, Chrome Store),
   guest posts, directories, and profile links.

## 5. The "money page" template (apply to every service + location page)

This is the structure that ranks AND converts in 2026. Order matters:

1. **H1** with primary keyword + location ("SEO Services in Kolkata that Bring You Customers")
2. **40–60 word direct answer** block (GEO — what AI engines quote)
3. **Trust bar** (5★, MSME, 18 yrs, clients-in-5-countries)
4. **Pain-point recognition** ("If your site gets visits but no calls…")
5. **What you get** — outcomes, not features (bullets/checklist)
6. **Your process** — 3–5 numbered steps (builds confidence)
7. **Proof** — 2–3 mini case studies with metrics + testimonial
8. **Transparent pricing range or "starting at"** (huge trust + qualifies leads)
9. **FAQ** (6–8 Q&As) with `FAQPage` schema — feeds "People also ask" + AI
10. **Primary CTA** (Book a call) + **secondary CTA** (lead magnet)
11. Schema: `Service` + `ProfessionalService` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` + `AggregateRating`

## 6. GEO / AI-search optimization (be the answer, not just a link)

For every important page and blog post:

- [ ] Put a **concise, self-contained answer** (40–60 words) directly under the H1.
- [ ] Use **clear headings phrased as questions** ("How much does a website cost in India?").
- [ ] Add **"Key takeaways"** bullet box near the top.
- [ ] Use **tables** for comparisons and **numbered steps** for processes (AI lifts these).
- [ ] Keep **`FAQPage` schema** accurate to on-page text.
- [ ] State **facts, dates, and specifics** (AI favors concrete, verifiable content).
- [ ] Ensure **entity clarity**: `Person`/`Organization` + `sameAs` + author bio on every post.
- [ ] Get **cited off-site** (WordPress.org profile, Chrome Store, LinkedIn articles) — AI weighs mentions.

## 8. Technical SEO checklist

- [ ] Submit/verify in **Google Search Console** + **Bing Webmaster Tools** (Bing feeds Copilot/ChatGPT).
- [ ] Keep `sitemap.xml` current; ping after major updates; ensure all new pages included.
- [ ] Check **Core Web Vitals** in Search Console + PageSpeed Insights (target LCP <2.5s, CLS <0.1, INP <200ms).
- [ ] Confirm every image has descriptive `alt` + keyword-relevant filename + lazy-load (you have `lazysizes`).
- [ ] Internal linking: every page ≤3 clicks from home; clusters interlinked.
- [ ] Fix any orphan pages; add contextual links from blog → services.
- [ ] Ensure mobile-first: responsive, tap targets ≥48px, no layout shift.
- [ ] Add `robots.txt` sitemap line (present ✅) and verify no accidental noindex.
- [ ] Descriptive, unique `<title>` (≤60 chars) + `meta description` (≤155 chars) per page, keyword-first.

## 9. Content cadence (sustainable for a solo operator)

Don't burn out. Consistency > volume.

- **Weeks 1–4:** Fix/expand 4 service pages + build 2 location pillars + `/free-seo-audit`. (No new blog yet.)
- **Then:** **1 high-quality post / week** targeting a Tier 3 keyword, each with a lead-magnet CTA.
- **Repurpose each post** into: 1 LinkedIn post, 1 YouTube short/idea (@viewanupam), 1 GBP post.
  One piece of content → 4 channels = maximum impressions for least effort.
- **Refresh** old posts quarterly (update dates, add GEO answer blocks) — refreshing often beats new.
