## The "least effort, best outcome" tool stack (near-zero cost)

Your prior plan budgeted **~$282/month**. This one targets **≈ $0–15/month**, because a solo
freelancer's constraint is *time*, not features. Every tool below works on static GitHub Pages.

| Job | Tool | Cost | Why |
|-----|------|------|-----|
| Form → your inbox | **Web3Forms** (or Formspree) | Free | Static-site friendly, no backend, spam filter, 250+ submits/mo free |
| Email list + automation | **MailerLite** | Free ≤1,000 subs | Automation on free tier; simpler than ConvertKit |
| Booking calls | **Cal.com** (or Calendly) | Free | Self-serve "book a 15-min call" |
| Live chat | **WhatsApp** (already integrated) + **Tawk.to** | Free | You already have WhatsApp; Tawk adds web chat |
| Heatmaps / recordings | **Microsoft Clarity** | Free | Replaces Hotjar ($89/mo) entirely |
| Analytics | **GA4** (installed) + **Search Console** + **Bing Webmaster** | Free | Already have GA4; add the search consoles |
| Lead tracking (CRM) | **Google Sheet** or **Notion** | Free | You don't need HubSpot yet |
| Automation glue | **Make.com** free tier (optional) | Free | Only if/when you want auto-routing |

**Total:** $0 to start. Add a paid tier only when a free limit actually bites.

## Non-negotiable principles for 2026 SEO (why this plan looks the way it does)

1. **Helpful-content + E-E-A-T first.** Google rewards first-hand *Experience*. You have 18 years and
   shipped products — show it. Thin/AI-spun pages lose.
2. **Answer engines matter now (GEO).** AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity cite well-structured,
   entity-clear, schema-rich pages. Optimize to *be quoted*, not just ranked.
3. **Local is your cheapest win.** "web developer in Kolkata"-type terms have real buyers and low
   competition vs. global "web developer."
4. **Entity clarity beats keyword stuffing.** One consistent identity (name, NAP, sameAs, `Person` +
   `LocalBusiness` schema) across the web makes you a recognizable entity.
5. **Conversion ≠ traffic.** No amount of ranking helps if the page has no capture. Do both.
