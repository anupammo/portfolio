3. CLIENT NEEDS & PROBLEM STATEMENT

Common Challenges Schools Face Today

A. Manual Record Keeping

  • Attendance marked in physical registers, then manually entered into spreadsheets
  • Student records stored across multiple files and locations
  • Risk of lost or damaged records
  • Difficulty finding historical data when needed
  • Time Cost: 8-12 hours/week

B. Fee Collection & Tracking Nightmare

  • Invoices issued manually or via email
  • No centralized payment status visibility
  • Overdue fees slip through the cracks
  • Parents unsure of payment status or outstanding amounts
  • Reconciliation errors between receipts and payments
  • Financial Impact: 10-15% fee collection delay

C. Poor Parent Communication

  • Important announcements sent via WhatsApp or email (gets lost)
  • Absence alerts reach parents hours or days later
  • Parents can't verify grades or attendance independently
  • No push notifications for urgent updates
  • Frustration on both ends—parents and school staff

D. Lack of Centralized Data

  • Student info in one system, attendance in another, grades in a third
  • Staff can't access required data quickly
  • Reports take hours or days to compile
  • Decision-making delayed due to information silos
  • No single source of truth for performance metrics

E. Limited Accessibility

  • Systems only accessible from the office
  • Teachers can't mark attendance from classroom
  • Parents need to visit school to check progress
  • Students have no real-time access to schedules or results

F. Compliance & Auditing Issues

  • No audit trail for financial transactions
  • Difficult to prove compliance with board regulations
  • Risk of data loss without backup systems
  • Security concerns with paper-based records

Why This Matters to Your School

Every day of manual operations means:

  • Reduced efficiency → fewer hours spent on strategic initiatives
  • Higher error rates → incorrect grades, attendance, or fee records
  • Lower parent satisfaction → complaints and reputation damage
  • Wasted resources → dedicated staff just managing spreadsheets
  • Missed growth opportunities → no insights into school performance
